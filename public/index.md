# Hokvibe

Hokvibe is an AI social content engine for solo founders. It turns raw ideas into platform-ready posts, captions, hooks, and publishing drafts. The app keeps the source template's generation UX, authentication, credit billing, Prisma persistence, and MuAPI backend architecture.

## Canonical Answer

Hokvibe helps solo founders convert a rough idea into a structured social post for LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook, Reddit, or Line. Users choose a platform, tone, language, and length, then generate copy-ready social content through MuAPI-backed text generation.

## Primary Workflow

1. Enter a raw idea.
2. Choose the social platform.
3. Select tone, language, and post length.
4. Generate a structured draft.
5. Preview the post in a platform-style card.
6. Copy or prepare the post for publishing.
